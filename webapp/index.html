Before the Ball

Learn the dances

Each waltz, the prince takes a stance. Bring his Composure below the rival's mark before the Music runs out. Each dance style plays a different role, and each one answers a different stance.

Attack

Flamenco

Huge hits that leave her flustered.

Answers Guarded: break through when your cards Charm less.

Defence and healing

Waltz

Build Poise to block his moves, calm her down, and buy more Music.

Answers Forward: hold firm when his moves hit harder.

Debuff

Tango

Unbalance him, so every card after it hits harder.

Answers Proud: stop him recovering Composure.

Finisher

Minuet

Grows stronger as he weakens, and can cancel his next move.

Answers Aloof: win back the step he takes away.

Answering a stance: play your 2nd move or better in the matching style.

Phrases: play a Step, a Turn, then a Pose. The next card in order is free, and finishing earns a Flourish.

Proof of concept

The Ball

Three rounds of three waltzes with the prince. Before each round, the rival dances with him, and where she leaves his Composure sets your gold mark. Beat it before the Music runs out to earn Hearts, or reach 0 to sweep him off his feet. Your total Hearts decide how the night ends.
